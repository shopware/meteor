## Usage

Use the header for what concerns the whole application: the brand at the start, a search across all of its content in the middle and the actions of the user at the end, such as help, notifications and the account. It stays above the content on every page.

Render the shell once, in the root component of your application. The header works on its own, as in this template, or together with a navigation in the `navigation` slot; see [**Sidebar navigation**](/blocks/application/sidebar-navigation). Replace the brand with your own and the `placeholder-slot` with the pages of your application.

## Components

- [**App**](/components/app) for the shell and its header
- [**Search**](/components/search) for the global search
- [**Button**](/components/button) and [**Icon**](/components/icon) for the actions
- [**User**](/components/user) for the account
- [**Page**](/components/page) for the page in the content
