## Usage

Use this shell for applications with a few top-level sections, which fit side by side in the header and leave the full width to the content. The brand sits at the start, the navigation next to it, and the actions and the account at the end. When the header gets narrow, the brand, the navigation and the account move into a drawer that opens from a button at its start, while the actions stay in the header.

Render the shell once, in the root component of your application, and the router view in its default slot. The template marks the current page with `active` on its item. With Vue Router, leave it out: **App Header** finds the current page from the route on its own. Replace the brand and the account with your own, and the `placeholder-slot` with the pages of your application. For more sections, put the navigation in a sidebar instead; see [**Sidebar navigation**](/blocks/application/sidebar-navigation).
