## Usage

Use this shell for applications with more than a handful of sections. The navigation stays visible next to the content and holds nested sections, with the brand above it and the account below it. On narrow screens, **App** turns the navigation into a drawer that opens from the header of the page.

Render the shell once, in the root component of your application, and the router view in its default slot. Replace the brand and the account with your own, and the `placeholder-slot` with the pages of your application.

The template marks the current page with `active` on its item. With Vue Router, leave it out: **App Sidebar** finds the current page from the route on its own.

The [**Panel**](/components/panel) around **App Sidebar** lets people resize the navigation between 200px and 320px by dragging its edge. Add `closable` to let them close it on desktop too.

## Examples

### With sidebar

The `sidebar` slot holds content that stays available on every page, next to the content, such as an assistant, a chat or the details of a selection. Its [**Panel**](/components/panel) names its trigger and its drawer, and makes it `resizable` and `closable`, so people open and close it from the trigger in the header of the page. Give the panel a `label` and an `icon` that name its content.

::block-preview{name="sidebar-navigation-sidebar-block"}
::

### With announcement

The `announcement` slot holds an [**Announcement**](/components/announcement) about the whole application, such as planned downtime, above everything else. When people close it, the template empties the slot, so the shell slides the announcement out and moves the focus on. In your application, keep it closed once they closed it. The button in the header of the page only brings it back, so you can try the template again.

::

## Components

- [**Brand**](/components/brand) for the name of the application
- [**User Menu**](/components/user-menu) for the account
