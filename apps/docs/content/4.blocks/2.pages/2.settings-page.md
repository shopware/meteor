## Usage

Use this page for the settings of an application, split into groups such as the basic information of a shop or its languages. The template above shows the slots it is built from: the actions of [**Page**](/components/page) next to its breadcrumb, its `start` panel for the groups, and the cards with the settings of the current group. On a narrow page, the panel turns into a drawer that opens from the header.

Keep the content narrow with `width="s"`, so the fields stay easy to read, and put the action that saves the settings in the header of the page. In your application, the shell is shared by all pages, so copy the **Page** into a route and the shell into your root component once.

## Examples

### With content

The groups in a [**Nav**](/components/nav) and the basic information of a shop in the cards. The navigation has its own `label`, because the page already has the main navigation of the shell. The template keeps a copy of the saved settings and compares it with the form, so **Save** and **Cancel** only become available after a change, and `useUnsavedChanges()` asks before a navigation or a reload discards the changes. Replace the sample data with your settings, and the timeout in `save()` with the request that saves them.

::block-preview{name="settings-page-content-block"}
::

### With the navigation in the layout

The groups in the `start` column of a [**Layout**](/components/layout) instead of the panel of the page. The column scrolls with the content, never becomes a drawer, and moves above the content on a narrow page. Use it for a few groups that should stay in the flow of the page, and the panel of the page for many groups or when the navigation should stay in place while the content scrolls. The page is wider here, with `width="m"`, so the column and the cards fit side by side.

## Components

- [**App**](/components/app) for the shell of the application
- [**Page**](/components/page) for the header, the content and the panel
- [**Panel**](/components/panel) or [**Layout**](/components/layout), and [**Nav**](/components/nav), for the groups of settings
- [**Content Header**](/components/content-header) for the name of the group
- [**Card**](/components/card) for the settings
- [**Text Field**](/components/text-field), [**Select**](/components/select) and [**Switch**](/components/switch) for the fields
- [**Button**](/components/button) to save or discard the changes
