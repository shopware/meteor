## Usage

Use this page for one record of a list, such as a product, a customer or an order. The template above shows the slots it is built from: the navigation of [**App**](/components/app), the actions of [**Page**](/components/page) next to its breadcrumb, the title and the details of [**Content Header**](/components/content-header), and the main column and the sidebar of [**Layout**](/components/layout).

The header of the page holds the actions that concern the whole record, such as saving. The main column holds the details of the record in cards, the sidebar the settings that are short to set, such as the visibility. In your application, the shell is shared by all pages, so copy the **Page** into a route and the shell into your root component once.

## Examples

### With content

A product with its fields in cards and tabs for its sections. The template keeps a copy of the saved record and compares it with the form, so **Save** and **Cancel** only become available after a change, and `useUnsavedChanges()` asks before a navigation or a reload discards the changes. Replace the sample data with your record, and the timeout in `save()` with the request that saves it.

## Components

- [**App**](/components/app) and [**Nav**](/components/nav) for the shell of the application
- [**Page**](/components/page) for the header with the breadcrumb and the actions
- [**Content Header**](/components/content-header) for the name and the status of the record
- [**Tabs**](/components/tabs) for the sections of the record
- [**Layout**](/components/layout) and [**Card**](/components/card) for the cards and the sidebar
- [**Text Field**](/components/text-field), [**Textarea**](/components/textarea), [**Number Field**](/components/number-field), [**Select**](/components/select) and [**Switch**](/components/switch) for the fields in the cards
