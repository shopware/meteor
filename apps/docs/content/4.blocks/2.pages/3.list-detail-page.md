## Usage

Use this page when people go through many records one after another, such as customers, messages or tickets, and should keep the list in view. The template above shows the slots it is built from: the `start` panel of [**Page**](/components/page) for the list, the actions next to the breadcrumb, and the [**Content Header**](/components/content-header) and the details of the selected record in the content. In the mobile layout of the page, the list turns into a drawer that opens from the header.

For records that need the whole width, or a list with many columns, use a list page that leads to a [**Detail page**](/blocks/pages/detail-page) instead. In your application, the shell is shared by all pages, so copy the **Page** into a route and the shell into your root component once.

## Examples

### With content

Customers with a search above the list and the contact details of the selected customer in cards. The list is a [**Nav**](/components/nav) with its own `label`, and `is-active` marks the selected record. The template keeps the selection in a ref, so it works without a router. With Vue Router, give each record its own route and leave out `is-active`: **Nav** then finds the selected record on its own. Replace the sample data with the records your page loads.

## Components
