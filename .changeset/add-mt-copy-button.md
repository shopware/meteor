Add the `mt-copy-button` component, an icon-only button that copies a value to the clipboard and confirms it with a checkmark, a "Copied" tooltip, and a live region announcement. The copy behavior is available on its own as the `useCopyToClipboard` composable, and `mt-tooltip` now exposes a `show` method to show the tooltip again after its trigger was clicked.
