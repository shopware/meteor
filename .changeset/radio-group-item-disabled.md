Add a `disabled` prop to `mt-radio-group-item`, `mt-radio-group-card-item` and `mt-radio-group-custom-item` to disable a single option of a radio group. The `mt-radio-group-indicator` inside a disabled `mt-radio-group-custom-item` is disabled as well, and the custom item passes the state to its default slot as `disabled`.
