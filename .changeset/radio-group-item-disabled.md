Add a `disabled` prop to `mt-radio-group-item`, `mt-radio-group-card-item` and `mt-radio-group-custom-item` to disable a single option of a radio group. `mt-radio-group-custom-item` passes the resulting state to its default slot as `disabled`, so it can be handed on to `mt-radio-group-indicator`.
